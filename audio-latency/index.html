Instructions:

  1. Connect your Bluetooth earbuds and select them as "Audio Output"
  2. Select built-in speakers as "Speaker Output" for baseline calibration
  3. Use a built-in microphone or USB mic (not Bluetooth) for "Audio Input"
  4. Click "Locate Mic" to play a tone and adjust your microphone position for a strong signal (green VU meter)
  5. Click "Calibrate Baseline" first using speakers + microphone
  6. Then click "Start Latency Test" with Bluetooth earbuds + microphone
